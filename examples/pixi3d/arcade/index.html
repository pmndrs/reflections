Reflections on pixi-3d

A neon arcade built with pixi-3d (@pixi/3d, closed beta) on pixi.js v8's WebGPURenderer. The signs are pixi Text and Graphics rendered into RenderTextures every frame and shown as the emissive maps of 3-D planes. pixi-3d draws the scene, its depth and its motion vectors; one extra pass draws normals and material values; @pmndrs/reflections/pixi runs core's screen-space tracer on pixi's device, with a probe captured by pixi-3d cameras and the sky as the fallback, and a pixi Filter composites the result. Drag to orbit. URL: ?view=, ?ssr=0, ?probes=0, ?denoise=0, ?variant=workgroup, ?manual.
