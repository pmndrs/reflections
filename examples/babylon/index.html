Reflections on Babylon.js (WebGPUEngine)

The mirrorWall scene rebuilt in Babylon. A frame graph renders colour (PBR, specular IBL stripped), depth, world normal, reflectivity and linear velocity; FrameGraphReflectionsTask runs the core screen-space tracer on Babylon's device with a core ProbeSet (one probe + the environment slot, captured by Babylon cube renders) as the radiance fallback; FrameGraphReflectionsCompositeTask adds radiance × Babylon's split-sum weight. URL: ?view=, ?ssr=0, ?probes=0, ?variant=workgroup, ?manual, ?scene=ibl (AO / normal-mapped metal, rough, clear-coat, sheen and bent-normal materials under a flat sky, to compare the composite with Babylon's own specular IBL; ?sky=gradient).
