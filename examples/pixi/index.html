Reflections on pixi.js v8 (WebGPURenderer)

A neon arcade. The signs are pixi Text, Graphics and sprites rendered into RenderTextures every frame; the adapter's 3-D layer lights them as emissive panels on a tiled floor (pixi has no G-buffer of its own). Core's screen-space tracer runs on pixi's device with a core ProbeSet (one probe + the sky) as the fallback, and a pixi Filter composites lit + split-sum × radiance. The inset is a second sprite with its own filter. Drag to orbit. URL: ?view=, ?ssr=0, ?probes=0, ?variant=workgroup, ?manual.
