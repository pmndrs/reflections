Reflection probes

Box-projected, GGX-prefiltered reflection probes from @pmndrs/reflections, live-captured as the scene changes: the torus knot spins and the left room's light cycles colour, and every reflection follows. The probe core (prefilter, layouts, the WGSL lookup) is engine-agnostic. The three adapter keeps the API of Ben Houston's three.js #35046; the two projects grew up together and have traded ideas and fixes throughout. On three r187 the cubes live in a 2D array with edge-centred texels until three can bind cube arrays.
