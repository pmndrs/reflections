| D5040 | SSSR / ray reuse (W73, X3 phase A, option; D50, D3740) | FFX SSSR classify + intersect (`samplesPerQuad` 1 / 2): one ray per 2x2 quad (or pair), its result copied to the other glossy pixels of the quad; its denoiser does the spatial reuse. SSSR 2015 (Stachowiak, Uludag) and DiligentFX (`SSR_SpatialReconstruction.fx:84`) resolve every pixel from a few neighbours' rays with the ratio estimator: each ray weighted by this pixel's BRDF over the ray's pdf, normalised. | Setting `rayReuse: 'copy' \| 'ratio'` (core `ScreenSpaceReflectionsSettings`, three `ssr.settings.rayReuse`, bench `s.rayReuse`), plus `rayReuseTaps` 4 \| 8 (default 8) and `rayReuseRadius` (px, default 4). 'copy' (default) is FFX bit for bit. 'ratio': intersect's storing variant writes a record per traced ray; a resolve pass after intersect (before the denoise seam, before the traced pass) writes radiance, confidence and hit distance of every glossy non-mirror pixel as sum(w x value) / sum(w) over its own ray, its quad's ray and the taps. A pixel with no usable ray keeps the copy. `Params.rayReuseFlags / Taps / Radius / Stamp` (words 150-153; PARAMS_SIZE 576 -> 640). Built on the first 'ratio' frame. | A sample that stands for a 2x2 quad shows as a 2x2 grid to TAA and the denoiser (D3663, T2, T10); reuse makes one ray per quad act like several (SSSR 2015: 1 ray + 4 reuse ~ 4 rays). Phase A of the plan (docs/plans/ssr-sources-experiments.md, X3). Phase 1 (docs/research/w73/ratio-reuse-phase1.md, 960x540): mirrorWall floor r 0.2/0.3/0.4 raw std 20.9/22.7/20.8 -> 11.2/14.3/15.1, but denoised std 0.41/0.48/0.48 -> 0.57/0.69/0.69 (copy@4 0.31/0.36/0.37): the resolve's error is blob-shaped and the FFX denoiser keeps it. carConcept (1280x720 hero) denoiser energy loss door / skirt 15 / 18 % -> 11 / 3 %. vs PT (w50, + RT, denoised) glossy 8.5 = 8.5, lip 41.7 -> 41.3, door 14.2 -> 13.8. Default-identical: hashes equal. | X0's metrics (2x2 energy, line spread, thin silhouettes, orbit flicker) are in: phase 2 decides the default and the promotion question. | option (off) |
| D5041 | SSSR / ray reuse weights (W73) | SSSR 2015: `localBRDF(hit) / hitPdf`, GGX NDF sampling with regeneration. Diligent: its own pdf output per ray (rgba16f dir x length, pdf). | w = D(h_p) G2(V_p, L) / (4 N_p.V_p) / [G1(V_q) D(h_q) / (4 N_q.V_q)]: this pixel's GGX lobe (separable Smith, no Fresnel: the split sum's DFG is applied after the denoiser) along L = the direction from this pixel to the neighbour's hit point (the ray's direction for a miss), over the VNDF pdf of the neighbour's own direction, recomputed from the neighbour's G-buffer (normal, alpha, view) instead of stored. The own ray's weight reduces to G1(L). `mirrorBias` truncation is ignored in the pdf (the untruncated VNDF pdf is used). | No pdf storage (the record stays 16 B/px); the G-buffer is at hand. | `mirrorBias` > 0 becomes a default (the truncated pdf is then wrong for the tail), or a stored pdf turns out cheaper. | option (off) |
| D5042 | SSSR / ray reuse rejection (W73; D3740) | SSSR 2015: none described beyond the BRDF weight. | D5044 | SSSR / ray reuse record (W73; D600, D304) | Diligent: rgba16f (hit colour, confidence) + rgba16f (dir x length, pdf) render targets per ray. | One vec4u per pixel in a W x H storage buffer (intersect is at 3 of 4 storage textures): pack2x16float(value.rg), pack2x16float(value.b, confidence), oct direction (2 x snorm16, as the miss list), f16 hit distance + a 15-bit dispatch stamp + the miss-listed bit. value = confidence x hit radiance with `fallbackRoughness` 'surface' (the resolve adds this pixel's own fallback for 1 - confidence), the whole mix(fallback, hit, confidence) with 'mirror'. f16 clamps at 65000. The stamp marks this frame's rays (no clear pass); `settings.frameIndex` doesn't drive it. 16 B/px: 33 MB at 1080p, fits the 128 MiB binding limit up to 3840 x 2160 (the resolve throws above). | One binding, no clear. | Memory matters (pack to 12 B, or index by quad at spq 1: 4 B/px), or above 4K. | option (off) |
| D5045 | SSSR / ray reuse outputs (W73) | SSSR 2015 / Diligent resolve every pixel, the traced ones too. FFX's radiance.w is the ray length. | Every glossy non-mirror pixel is resolved, the ones with their own ray too (sum includes the own ray). Confidence = sum(w c) / sum(w); hit distance = sum(w c t') / sum(w c) over hits, t' = the distance from this pixel to the neighbour's hit (re-derived, not the neighbour's own t); 0 with no hit. radiance.w = that hit distance (no ray length for the resolved pixels). | Confidence and hit distance must follow the radiance (D2400's depth-aware blend and the denoiser's reprojection read them). | A consumer reads radiance.w as the ray length. | option (off) |
| D5047 | SSSR / ray reuse and the depth-aware blend (W73; D3660, D2400) | SSSR 2015 / Diligent mix every neighbour's ray (hits and misses: a miss is black with confidence 0 in Diligent, alpha filtered separately). | By default the resolve reuses only rays of the pixel's own hit source: screen hit (confidence > 0) vs miss, the source being its own ray's, else its quad's ray's (what the copy would give it); a pixel with neither takes any. `rayReuseMixSources: true` (flag bit 2, bench `s.rayReuseMixSources`) mixes them. | Mixed, confidence becomes the share of hits around the pixel (~0.27 on carConcept's door instead of 0 / ~1 per quad), and the three composite's depth-aware blend (D2400, w = max(c, v (1 - (1 - c/v) k))) reads it as one ray's thickness confidence and gives the hits the whole weight: door raw signal (SSR only, 1280x720 hero, linear, 32 frames) 1.043 copy -> 1.376 mixed, 1.043 with `depthAware` 0, **1.065 kept apart**. The tracer's own means were unchanged (confidence 0.266 / 0.269, radiance 0.371 / 0.378). Kept apart, the hit / miss pattern is not smoothed: roughnessRamp r 0.2 raw std 9.78 copy, 7.98 apart, 4.65 mixed. | A composite linear in confidence (or the depth-aware rule applied per ray in the resolve) would let the mixed form's extra noise reduction through unbiased. | option (off) |
