| D5061 | RT / bilateral fill of untraced misses (W74, X4; reused by X5) | Kopf et al. 2007 (JBU): spatial Gaussian over low-res taps x a range Gaussian on a full-res colour guide, ~5x5. Game variants: geometric range weights over a bilateral 2x2 with a nearest-depth fallback. | Core `bilateralFill` (`packages/core/src/bilateral/fill.ts`): an includer-defined tap grid (X4: 2x2 quads; X5: half-res texels), footprint 2 / 3 / 4 cells, weight = spatial (includer's; X4 flat 1) x saturate(1 - plane distance / (0.01 x view distance)) x pow(dot(n_p, n_q), 16) x saturate(1 - \|dr\| / 0.1) (perceptual) x source mask (X4 default: RT_SOURCE traced hit, traced miss, on-screen traced hit; not screen-space hits, not capped). Normalised; radiance (+ ray length), hit distance and confidence with the same weights; the source written is the one with the largest weight share. Weight sum < 0.01: the pixel keeps intersect's probe fallback and source 'capped' (as an entry past the ray budget). Hard-zero tent weights instead of Gaussians, so a silhouette gives exactly 0. | X4 contact 960x540: 14.7k px filled, 573 kept the probe (3.9 %). | X5 builds its upsample on it; tune the tolerances with X0's metrics. | option (with D5060) |
| D5062 | RT / fill passes (W74) | — | Three passes after the trace, one thread per (fill entry, quad pixel): mark (source = fillPending 5 on every fill pixel, so fill pixels never tap each other), gather (reads the targets sampled, writes a 16 B/px scratch: rgba16 radiance, f32 hit distance, 24-bit confidence + source), scatter (writes the targets). Two passes because a target can't be sampled and written in one dispatch and rgba16float has no read-write storage in core WebGPU. Scratch = fill capacity x 64 B (1080p 16.6 MB), made on first 'half' use. RT Params words 68-74 (RT_PARAMS_SIZE 320), state words 12-13 (filled / fallbacks). | carConcept 1080p: mark 0.014, gather 0.079, scatter 0.032 ms at 52.6k px; at 481k px 0.056 / 0.38 / 0.41 ms. | Scatter is slow at large fill counts (0.41 ms for 481k px): fold mark into the trace pass, or write the gather's result straight into r32float targets with read-write access and only radiance through the scratch. | measured |
