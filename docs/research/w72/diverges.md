| D5020 | SSSR / cone-footprint hit colour (W72, X2 #5; D3550) | FFX SSSR returns the hit texel of this frame's lit colour (or the hybrid sample's history) as is. SSSR15 (Stachowiak & Uludag 2015) reads a pre-convolved colour pyramid at the mip of the lobe cone's footprint (roughness x hit distance), shrunk at grazing views; Colbert & Krivanek 2007 prefilter each sample by its solid angle. | Setting `hitColorFilter: 'off' (default) \| 'cone'` + `hitColorConeScale` (default 1); option `hitColorPyramid` (three and pixi rebuild the tracer on a flip). An 8-level rgba16float pyramid (6 at first; 8 since the cap test) of the hit-colour source (this frame's scene colour without specular; last frame's for `hitColor: 'history'` with `hitShading: 'off'`), built each frame by the maintainer's `downsampler` (level 0 a converting copy, then 2x2 averages). At a hit: cone full angle = the reflected GGX lobe's FWHM, 2.574 alpha (half-vector HWHM tan 0.6436 alpha, doubled) x scale x the filter share (D5021); width = 2 rayLength tan(angle / 2), projected at the hit's depth; mip = log2(px), trilinear; below mip 0 the texel as before (contacts, mirrors). With the re-shade it filters the hit's diffuse (screen) part only. No grazing shrink (X1 #3), no depth-aware pyramid, not applied to the RT pass's on-screen reused hits (RT_SOURCE 4). Intersect +1 sampled texture +1 sampler (D4900 count 10 -> 11). Cost at 1080p: pyramid pass +0.24-0.26 ms, intersect within noise. | Raw fireflies from single-texel hits. Raw, 32 frames (default -> cone): mirrorWall r 0.35 sphere relStd 0.79 -> 0.56, p999 23.9 -> 4.0 x mean, mean -3 %; pixi3d arcade floor relStd 0.43 -> 0.31, fireflies 2.7 -> 1.25 %; carConcept paint unchanged (its variance is the re-shaded specular). W45 energy (door / skirt denoiser loss) unchanged. Default off is bit for bit (examples/ssr 9 scenes raw + denoised, showcase carConcept; `--only hit-color-filter`). | X0's line-spread gate (±10 % of the PT FWHM at r 0.05-0.5): plain 'cone' at scale 1 is a x1.41 double blur on the screen part like `hitLobeFilter` 1, expected to fail unless the denoiser hides it. Leakage at depth edges (plain pyramid). X1 #3's grazing shrink when it lands (the denoised grazing floor is blurrier with the cone: gradient x0.40 vs 0.79). A cheaper pyramid. | measured (W72 phase 2): line spread r 0.05 x1.24 -> 1.22 (no widening), r >= 0.15 still lost alone (raw AA off loses r 0.15: the full-lobe cone over-spreads a thin emitter); grazing raw MAD 32 -> 8 at 5 deg; no leakage at silhouettes (edge MAD +0.13); 8 levels: sphere fireflies 1.72 -> 1.22 %. Verdict: keep as option, default off; use with D5022. |
| D5021 | SSSR + RT / the double-blur budget (W72, X2; D3550, D3641) | Colbert & Krivanek / SSSR15 prefilter every sample by its cone without subtracting the spread the sampling already covers; D3550 (`hitLobeFilter` 1) does the same, a x1.41 alpha double blur. | With `mirrorBiasCompensate` (and 'cone'): widths add in squares, the sample covers alpha sqrt(1 - f), every filter covers alpha sqrt(f) (`mirrorBiasSplit`), so alpha_s^2 + alpha_f^2 = alpha^2. The cone (screen-colour part) and `hitLobeFilter` (specular part) split the hit and don't stack on one term; both use the same share: the lobe filter's widening becomes hitLobeFilter x alpha x sqrt(f) in the SSSR re-shade (Params word 149) and in the RT pass (RT word 76, `TracedReflectionsSettings.mirrorBias / mirrorBiasCompensate / hitColorFilter`, the three adapter passes the tracer's). Without compensation both filters keep their full width (today's double blur). Derivation: docs/research/w72/cone-hit-colour.md. | Make `mirrorBias` honest (D3550's revisit): less noise without narrowing the lobe. Without shrinking the lobe filter the specular part would be sqrt(1 - f + k^2) alpha (1.14 at f 0.7, k 1). Cost seen: carConcept paint's re-shaded specular gets less filtering, p999 17 -> 20-28 x mean, mean -2..-5 %; W45 skirt loss 18.5 % -> 26.4 / 19.7 / 13.8 % at f 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7. | X1 #3's grazing shrink; whether k > 1 is wanted under compensation for paint-like surfaces. | measured (W72 phase 2): see D5022 |
| D5022 | SSSR / mirror bias by narrowing, not truncation (W72, X2 #6; D49, D3300) | SSSR15 truncates its GGX NDF sampler (`u.x = lerp(u.x, 1, bias)`, shipped 0.7) and pairs it with the cone colour; ours (D49) truncates Heitz's VNDF radius (U1 x (1 - f)) with no paired blur. | Setting `mirrorBiasCompensate: boolean` (default false; ignored without `hitColorFilter: 'cone'`): the VNDF is sampled at alpha x sqrt(1 - f) with U1 untouched (Params word 148; `select` keeps D49's truncation when off), and the cone + lobe filter take alpha x sqrt(f) (D5021). | Narrowing keeps the GGX shape (its tail), so the filter of the same shape restores the full lobe in the sum-of-squares model; truncation cuts the tail, which no GGX-shaped blur puts back.
