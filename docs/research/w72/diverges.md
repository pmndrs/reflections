| D5020 | SSSR / cone-footprint hit colour (W72, X2 #5; D3550) | FFX SSSR returns the hit texel of this frame's lit colour (or the hybrid sample's history) as is. SSSR15 (Stachowiak & Uludag 2015) reads a pre-convolved colour pyramid at the mip of the lobe cone's footprint (roughness x hit distance), shrunk at grazing views; Colbert & Krivanek 2007 prefilter each sample by its solid angle. | Setting `hitColorFilter: 'off' (default) \| 'cone'` + `hitColorConeScale` (default 1); option `hitColorPyramid` (three and pixi rebuild the tracer on a flip). A 6-level rgba16float pyramid of the hit-colour source (this frame's scene colour without specular; last frame's for `hitColor: 'history'` with `hitShading: 'off'`), built each frame by the maintainer's `downsampler` (level 0 a converting copy, then 2x2 averages). At a hit: cone full angle = the reflected GGX lobe's FWHM, 2.574 alpha (half-vector HWHM tan 0.6436 alpha, doubled) x scale x the filter share (D5021); width = 2 rayLength tan(angle / 2), projected at the hit's depth; mip = log2(px), trilinear; below mip 0 the texel as before (contacts, mirrors). With the re-shade it filters the hit's diffuse (screen) part only. No grazing shrink (X1 #3), no depth-aware pyramid, not applied to the RT pass's on-screen reused hits (RT_SOURCE 4). Intersect +1 sampled texture +1 sampler (D4900 count 10 -> 11). | Raw fireflies from single-texel hits. W45 energy (door / skirt denoiser loss) unchanged. Default off is bit for bit (examples/ssr 9 scenes raw + denoised, showcase carConcept; `--only hit-color-filter`). | X0's line-spread gate (±10 % of the PT FWHM at r 0.05-0.5): plain 'cone' at scale 1 is a x1.41 double blur on the screen part like `hitLobeFilter` 1, expected to fail unless the denoiser hides it. Leakage at depth edges (plain pyramid). The 32 px cap (6 levels): the sphere wants ~39 px. X1 #3's grazing shrink when it lands. GPU ms of the extra downsample. | phase 1 (built, first numbers; gate pending) |
| D5021 | SSSR + RT / the double-blur budget (W72, X2; D3550, D3641) | Colbert & Krivanek / SSSR15 prefilter every sample by its cone without subtracting the spread the sampling already covers; D3550 (`hitLobeFilter` 1) does the same, a x1.41 alpha double blur. | With `mirrorBiasCompensate` (and 'cone'): widths add in squares, the sample covers alpha sqrt(1 - f), every filter covers alpha sqrt(f) (`mirrorBiasSplit`), so alpha_s^2 + alpha_f^2 = alpha^2. The cone (screen-colour part) and `hitLobeFilter` (specular part) split the hit and don't stack on one term; both use the same share: the lobe filter's widening becomes hitLobeFilter x alpha x sqrt(f) in the SSSR re-shade (Params word 149) and in the RT pass (RT word 76, `TracedReflectionsSettings.mirrorBias / mirrorBiasCompensate / hitColorFilter`, the three adapter passes the tracer's). Without compensation both filters keep their full width (today's double blur). Derivation: docs/research/w72/cone-hit-colour.md. | Make `mirrorBias` honest (D3550's revisit): less noise without narrowing the lobe. Without shrinking the lobe filter the specular part would be sqrt(1 - f + k^2) alpha (1.14 at f 0.7, k 1). Cost seen: carConcept paint's re-shaded specular gets less filtering, p999 17 -> 20-28 x mean, mean -2..-5 %; W45 skirt loss 18.5 % -> 26.4 / 19.7 / 13.8 % at f 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7. | The line-spread gate (phase 2): if the honest split under-blurs the specular part vs the PT, keep k > 1 under compensation (a separate knob). | phase 1 |
